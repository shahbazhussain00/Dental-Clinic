What we treat

General, cosmetic, and urgent care — most of it booked within the week.

  • Cleanings & exams

    Twice-yearly cleanings, digital X-rays, and a straight answer about what actually needs attention.

    Every 6 months

  • Emergency care

    Chipped tooth, sudden pain, lost filling — call before 2pm and we'll usually see you same day.

    Same-day slots

  • Clear aligners

    Straightening for adults and teens, mapped out with a 3D scan — no putty impressions.

    12–18 months

  • Whitening

    In-chair whitening in about an hour, or a take-home tray kit if you'd rather do it on your couch.

    1 visit or at-home

  • Kids & teens

    First visits, sealants, and sports guards — with a chair-side approach built for shorter attention spans.

    From age 3

  • Root canals & crowns

    Root canal therapy and same-visit same-day crowns, milled in our own lab down the hall.

    1–2 visits
